Vertical Deformation Analysis of The Northern Coastal of Central Java using GNSS CORS And InSAR Data

Analyzed vertical deformation along the northern coast of Central Java using GNSS CORS and Sentinel-1 InSAR data from 2022–2024. Processed GNSS observations with GAMIT/GLOBK and performed InSAR time-series analysis using LiCSBAS. The study identified subsidence and uplift patterns and compared deformation trends between GNSS and InSAR, highlighting their complementary capabilities for monitoring coastal deformation.

Analysis of GNSS Coordinate Solutions using Precise Point Positioning and Network-based Processing Methods

Compared GNSS coordinate solutions from PRIDE PPP-AR and GAMIT/GLOBK using CORS and campaign observations from 2017–2022 around the Kendeng Fault. The study evaluated annual coordinate differences and their statistical significance, showing generally consistent results between the two processing methods.

The manuscript has submitted on Journal of Applied Geodesy
